### 2. First-Principles Apriori Architecture

To ensure complete transparency for academic examination and thesis defense, the Apriori algorithm was built directly from foundational mathematical principles without black-box machine learning libraries:

```
[Transactions Matrix] 
         │
         ▼
[C1 Candidate 1-Itemsets] ──(Count Support)──► [L1 Frequent 1-Itemsets (min_support >= 0.10)]
         │
         ▼ (Self-Join L1 ⋈ L1)
[C2 Candidates (28 pairs)] ──(Prune + Count)──► [L2 Frequent 2-Itemsets (7 pairs)]
         │
         ▼ (Self-Join L2 ⋈ L2)
[C3 Candidates (Join + Apriori Pruning)] ──► [All 3-itemsets pruned: |C3| = 0] ──► STOP
         │
         ▼
[Rule Generation from L2] ──(Confidence >= 0.20)──► [13 Association Rules]
```

#### Key Algorithmic Mechanics:
1. **The Apriori Anti-Monotonicity Property:** Any subset of a frequent itemset must also be frequent ($S \subset I \land I \in L_k \implies S \in L_{k-1}$).
2. **Candidate Pruning Step:** For any candidate itemset $c \in C_k$, generate all of its $(k-1)$-subsets. If any subset is not present in $L_{k-1}$, $c$ is immediately pruned without scanning the transaction matrix.
3. **Rule Formation:** For every frequent itemset $I \in L_k$ ($k \ge 2$), generate non-empty proper subsets $A \subset I$. If $\text{Confidence}(A \to I \setminus A) \ge \text{min\_confidence}$, retain the rule and compute Lift.

#### Iteration 3 ($k=3$): The Apriori Pruning Demonstration
When candidate 3-itemsets were generated by joining pairs from $L_2$:
- Example candidate: `{"CLEANSER", "MAKEUP_REMOVAL", "TONER"}`.
- Subsets of size 2:
  - `{"CLEANSER", "MAKEUP_REMOVAL"}` $\in L_2$ (count 7)
  - `{"CLEANSER", "TONER"}` $\in L_2$ (count 6)
  - `{"MAKEUP_REMOVAL", "TONER"}`: Has count 2 ($6.67\% < 10.0\%$), so `{"MAKEUP_REMOVAL", "TONER"} \notin L_2$.
- Because a $(k-1)$-subset is missing from $L_2$, the Apriori pruning property immediately drops `{"CLEANSER", "MAKEUP_REMOVAL", "TONER"}`.
- Every other candidate triplet similarly contained at least one infrequent 2-subset.
- As a result, **zero candidates survived pruning ($|C_3| = 0$)**, proving that Apriori prevented unnecessary database scans.

### 6. Directional Asymmetry Verification

Comparing forward and reverse rules on the exact same transactions:

| Forward Rule | Support | Conf | Lift | Reverse Rule | Support | Conf | Lift | Symmetry Analysis |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |

**Empirical Proof for Defense:** $\text{Support}(A \cup B)$ and $\text{Lift}(A \to B)$ are invariant under direction reversal, but $\text{Confidence}(A \to B) \ne \text{Confidence}(B \to A)$ due to differing base antecedent frequencies ($N(A) \ne N(B)$).

#### Key Trade-Offs for Thesis Discussion:
1. **Low Thresholds (Exp A):** Yields more rules ($43$), but introduces $11$ weak or accidental associations ($\text{Lift} \le 1$).

3. **No Universal Optimum:** Threshold selection depends on the system objective (precision vs coverage).

### 10. Step-by-Step Defense Trace: `MAKEUP_REMOVAL -> CLEANSER`

1. **Transaction Count:** Total $N = 30$.
2. **Marginal Counts:** $N(\text{MAKEUP\_REMOVAL}) = 8$, $N(\text{CLEANSER}) = 17$, $N(\text{both}) = 7$.
3. **Apriori Execution Trace:**
   - **Step A ($L_1$ Discovery):** $\text{Support}(\text{MAKEUP\_REMOVAL}) = 8/30 = 0.2667 \ge 0.10 \implies$ retained in $L_1$. $\text{Support}(\text{CLEANSER}) = 17/30 = 0.5667 \ge 0.10 \implies$ retained in $L_1$.
   - **Step B ($C_2$ Candidate Generation):** Both items are joined to form candidate pair $c = \{\text{CLEANSER}, \text{MAKEUP\_REMOVAL}\}$.
   - **Step C (Pruning Verification):** Both single-item subsets are in $L_1$. Candidate survives pruning.
   - **Step D ($L_2$ Retention):** Transaction matrix scan yields count = 7. $\text{Support} = 7/30 = 0.2333 \ge 0.10 \implies$ retained in $L_2$.
   - **Step E (Directional Rule Formulation):** Construct rule $\text{MAKEUP\_REMOVAL} \to \text{CLEANSER}$.
   - **Step F (Confidence Evaluation):** $\text{Confidence} = 7 / 8 = 0.8750$ ($87.5\% \ge 0.20$) $\implies$ rule accepted.
   - **Step G (Lift Calculation):** $\text{Lift} = \frac{0.8750}{17/30} = \frac{7 \times 30}{8 \times 17} = \mathbf{1.5441} > 1.0$ (Positive association).
